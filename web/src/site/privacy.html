Privacy policy

boh is a static web page published by Binary Dice Games. It has no server of its own: it runs entirely in your browser and talks directly to your Google Drive.

What boh accesses

  • Your Google account name and email address, to show which account is connected.
  • The folders you register as bins and the files in them, to list, upload, show and delete pastes when you ask it to. In Google Drive, boh only sees files it created and files and folders you choose for it in Google's picker.
  • One settings file (boh-config.json) in Google Drive's private app-data area, holding your list of bins.

What boh stores and sends

  • Nothing on any server of Binary Dice Games: there is none. Your data goes only between your browser and Google.
  • In your browser: sign-in tokens (in local storage only when you choose to stay signed in), a copy of your settings, the pastes you opened or created recently, and display preferences. Disconnecting an account removes what boh stored for it.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Pictures linked from inside a paste are only loaded when you ask for them.

Who can see your pastes

Whoever Google Drive lets see the folder or file. boh never widens the sharing of a folder; it makes per-paste links that match the visibility you chose for the bin.

Use of Google user data

boh's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data is used only to provide the features above and is never transferred to others.
